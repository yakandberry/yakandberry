Our Craft

Three things we never compromise on.

Brought to you by Highland Dairy Co. under the management of Mithu Kitchen Pvt. Ltd., every cup, carton, and can traces back to our highland philosophy: source honestly, culture slowly, and sweeten with real fruit.

Highland Milk

Cream that starts richer

Our dairy comes from herds grazed on mineral-rich alpine pasture, giving every batch a naturally fuller, creamier body before we add a single ingredient.

Wildcrafted Berries

Fruit picked at its peak

Strawberries, blackberries, and wild blueberries are folded in ripe — never from concentrate — so every swirl tastes like the berry it came from.

Slow-Cultured

No shortcuts, ever

We give our cultures the time they need. It's slower than the industry standard, and it's the reason our yogurt holds its thickness, spoon after spoon.
